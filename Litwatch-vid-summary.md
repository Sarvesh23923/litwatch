## What the video is about

> "**Built for consultants. Designed for speed. Powered by AI. Controlled by you.**"

## Key Sections Covered

- **Notice Ingestion & Parsing** — Ingests notices from multiple input sources and document types, then structures/parses them.
- **Metadata Capture** — Extracts metadata such as person/client, key dates, deadlines, and issuing authority/institution.
- **Classification & Deadline Extraction** — AI classifies notice type, extracts deadlines, and pulls matching GST portal data.
- **Validation & Reconciliation** — Compares notice data against filings/records, flags matches vs. mismatches, and generates a compliance/confidence score.
- **Draft Reply Generation** — Combines legal/rule reasoning with validated findings to auto-generate a draft response; routed for **human review and approval** (with edit/override controls) via a Reviewer Dashboard.
- **Tracking** — Tracks hearings, orders, appeal status, and recovery risk end-to-end.
- **Next Action Decisions** — Outcomes branch into paths like payment, formal reply/appeal, or certification/compliance record.
- **MVP Roadmap** (4 phases):

  2. Section 73 & 74 notices support
  3. Reply drafts (AI-generated, editable)
  4. Prioritization & control tower (dashboards, compliance monitoring)
